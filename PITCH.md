# Signal — product pitch

Signal is a market watchlist for people who track a lot of stocks but check in occasionally, not all day. 
A normal watchlist just shows prices , you still scan every row yourself to spot what matters. 
Signal flags a stock only when its move is unusual for that stock: unusual price movement, unusual volume, unusual relative to the market and only when multiple signals line up together, not on any single number crossing a threshold. Every flag lists its exact reasons. Absence is handled too: return after a day or six months, and Signal still shows only what's genuinely worth your attention.
